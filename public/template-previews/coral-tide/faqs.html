Booking and payment

Is my booking confirmed when I send the form?

Not yet. The form sends us a booking request. We check availability and email you to confirm within 4 hours (8am to 10pm).

When do I pay?

You pay at the resort, at check-in or check-out. When we confirm your booking we'll ask for a card to guarantee it.

Can I cancel?

On flexible rates you can cancel free of charge up to 48 hours before arrival. Read our booking terms.

Your stay
