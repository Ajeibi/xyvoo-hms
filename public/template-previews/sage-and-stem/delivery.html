How long it takes

We pack orders placed before 1pm on the same working day. Standard delivery takes 2 to 4 working days, and next-day orders placed before 1pm arrive the following working day.

On the day

  • You'll get an email with a tracking link as soon as your parcel leaves our studio.
  • Parcels fit through most letterboxes. If yours doesn't, the courier will leave a card.
  • Our packaging is paper-based and fully recyclable.
