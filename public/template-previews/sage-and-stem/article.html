A simple evening routine for dry skin

Three steps, ten minutes, and skin that feels comfortable by morning.

By the Sage & Stem team10 September 20265 minute read

Article hero photo

Dry skin doesn't need a twelve-step routine. It needs the right few steps, done consistently. This is the routine we recommend most often in the studio.

1. Cleanse gently

Use a cream cleanser rather than a foaming one. Massage it in with dry hands, then remove with a warm, damp cloth.

2. Treat while skin is damp

A few drops of serum or face oil go further, and sink in better, on skin that is still slightly damp.

3. Seal it in

Finish with a richer night cream. It protects your skin's barrier while you sleep.

“Consistency beats complexity. Three steps every night will do more than ten steps once a week.”
