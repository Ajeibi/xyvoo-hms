Booking and payment

Is my booking confirmed when I send the form?

Not yet. The form sends us a booking request. We check availability and email you to confirm within one working day.

When do I pay?

Nothing is charged when you send a request. When we confirm, we'll email a secure link for a [25%] deposit, with the balance due 14 days before arrival.

Can I cancel?

You can cancel free of charge up to 7 days before arrival. Read our booking terms.

Your stay

What time are check-in and check-out?

Check-in is from 4pm and check-out is by 10am. Ask about early check-in or late check-out when you book.

Can I bring my dog?

Yes. Up to two dogs are welcome in our dog-friendly lodges for [£20 per dog, per stay]. Add them when you book.

Do you welcome children?

Families are very welcome. Travel cots and high chairs are free; just ask when you book.

Do you have accessible lodges?

Yes. See our accessibility information or tell us what you need when you book.

Getting here

Is there parking?

Free parking next to every lodge, and [6] electric vehicle chargers at reception.
