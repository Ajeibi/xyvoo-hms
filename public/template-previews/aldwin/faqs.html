Booking and payment

Is my booking confirmed when I send the form?

Not yet. The form sends us a booking request. We check availability and email you to confirm within 2 hours (7am to 11pm).

When do I pay?

You pay at the hotel when you check in. When we confirm your booking we'll ask for a card to hold the room.

Can I cancel?

On flexible rates you can cancel free of charge until 2pm the day before arrival. Read our booking terms.

Your stay

What time are check-in and check-out?

Check-in is from 3pm and check-out is by 12pm. Ask about early check-in or late check-out when you book.

Can I bring my dog?

Assistance dogs are always welcome. Other pets can stay in selected rooms for [£20 a night]; please ask first.

Do you welcome children?

Yes. Our Family Room sleeps four, and cots are free on request.

Do you have accessible rooms?

Yes. See our accessibility information or tell us what you need when you book.
