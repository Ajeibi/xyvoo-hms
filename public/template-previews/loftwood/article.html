How to choose the right sofa size

Measure once, buy once. A quick guide to getting the size right for your room.

By the Loftwood team15 September 20265 minute read

Article hero photo

The most common reason people return a sofa is size. Here's how to make sure yours fits your room, and your life.

1. Measure the space, then the route

Measure where the sofa will sit, then every doorway, corridor and stairway it needs to pass through. Every product page lists the narrowest gap needed.

2. Leave room to move

Aim for at least 75 cm of walkway around the sofa, and 40 to 45 cm between the sofa and a coffee table.

3. Choose the right depth

Deep seats are great for lounging; shallower seats suit sitting upright. If you're shorter, a depth of around 55 cm is often more comfortable.

“Tape it out on the floor. It takes five minutes and saves a return.”
