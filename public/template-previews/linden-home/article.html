Five small changes for a calmer living room

You don't need a full makeover. A few considered swaps can make a room feel quieter, lighter and easier to live in.

By the Linden Home team1 September 20266 minute read

Article hero photo

A calm room isn't an empty room. It's one where everything has a place, the light is soft, and nothing is shouting for your attention. Here are five changes that make the biggest difference for the least effort.

1. Edit before you add

Take everything off one surface and only put back what you use or love. Most rooms feel calmer with a third fewer objects on show.

2. Layer your lighting

Swap one bright ceiling light for two or three lamps at different heights. A floor lamp by the sofa and a small table lamp on a sideboard is enough to change the mood of a room in the evening.

3. Choose texture over pattern

Linen, wool and oiled wood add interest without visual noise. Keep colours close together and let texture do the work.

“Let the materials speak. A room of natural textures never feels flat.”

4. Hide the everyday

A sideboard or closed bookshelf keeps chargers, post and remotes out of sight but close to hand.

5. Make one seat the best seat

Put your most comfortable chair where the light is best, with a small table beside it. It becomes the place you go to slow down.
